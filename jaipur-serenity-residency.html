About Project

Serenity Residency – a premium township, inspired by the culture of the North East. Spanning across 100,000 sq. mtr., thoughtfully designed to offer both residential plots and retail shops, blending modern convenience with soulful serenity.

This premium project by Riyasat Infra blends traditional aesthetics with modern living for those who look for harmony, wellness, and connection with nature. Whether it’s healthcare, education, industry or recreation, everything is within easy reach.

At the heart of this township lies a celebration of life and calm. Residents are welcomed by an elegant entrance foyer and can indulge in an active lifestyle with amenities like an indoor gym, Zumba zone, game zone, basketball court and relay track. For children, the kids&#x27; play area, Little Smile Park with traditional gongs and engaging pathways offers joy and play in a safe environment.
