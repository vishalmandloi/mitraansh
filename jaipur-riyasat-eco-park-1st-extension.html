About Project

Riyasat Eco Park is a thoughtfully planned township designed with the essence of Jaipur’s rich heritage, following the charming Gulabi Nagari theme. Spread across 100,000 sq.mt, this JDA &amp; RERA-approved project offers a perfect blend of modern living and cultural aesthetics. With residential plots and retail shops, best-in-class infrastructure, and wide roads, it ensures a seamless living experience. The township is well-connected to key landmarks like Jaipur International Airport, Ring Road, and Sanganer Railway Station. Residents will enjoy top-notch amenities such as a Govind Dev Ji Temple, community hall, sports courts, parks, and open-air gym, making it an ideal place to call home.

Riyasat Residency and Vatika Residency are exceptional townships in Jaipur, approved by the Jaipur Development Authority and registered under RERA. Offering affordable rates, a prime location, excellent development, and over a decade of experience in successful projects, these townships are designed to make your dream home a reality. Enjoy a wide range of amenities, including flower beds, a Hindu temple, kids’ fun zones, meditation areas, community halls, and much more for a perfect living experience.
