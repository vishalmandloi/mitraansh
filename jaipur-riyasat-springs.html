About Project

Riyasat Springs is a well-developed township spread across 4,112 sq. m, thoughtfully designed for your comfort and convenience. This JDA-approved and RERA-registered township brings together modern architecture and world-class infrastructure to improve your everyday living experience.

Every amenity here is carefully planned to make life easier and more accessible -from a dedicated shopping area for your daily needs to lush green spaces that promote health, wellness, and a peaceful lifestyle.

Explore the walkthrough video of The Park Residency, a thoughtfully planned township that blends Jaipur&#x27;s rich heritage with the charm of the Gulabi Nagari theme. With easy connectivity to key landmarks and amenities like a Govind Dev Ji Temple, sports courts, parks, and an open-air gym, it’s the perfect place to call home.
