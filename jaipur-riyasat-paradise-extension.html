About Project

Welcome to Riyasat Paradise Extension -a JDA-approved and RERA-registered township spread across 53,890 sq. m., The township includes well-planned internal roads of 30 m, 18 m, 12 m, and 9 m width, ensuring smooth connectivity.

Experience a life of comfort and elegance with world-class amenities at your fingertips. The township offers a dedicated Kids’ Play Area where children can play, learn, and grow in a safe environment, beautifully tiled internal roads, an Open-Air Theatre for community gatherings and entertainment, and solar panels for common areas, promoting sustainable and eco-friendly living.

Riyasat Residency and Vatika Residency are exceptional townships in Jaipur, approved by the Jaipur Development Authority and registered under RERA. Offering affordable rates, a prime location, excellent development, and over a decade of experience in successful projects, these townships are designed to make your dream home a reality. Enjoy a wide range of amenities, including flower beds, a Hindu temple, kids’ fun zones, meditation areas, community halls, and much more for a perfect living experience.
