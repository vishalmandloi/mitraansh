About Project

Spread across 1.5690 hectares, this Vastu-compliant township offers residential plots &amp; retail shops with best-in-class infrastructure and a serene flower-themed development. Enjoy top-tier amenities like a temple, community hall, landscape park, gazebo, deer park, jogging track, wellness center, open discussion zone, skywalk, and herbal garden. Well-planned 4.5m, 9m &amp; 12m roads ensure smooth connectivity, with DMART (5.5 km) and Chokhi Dhani (1 km) nearby.

A perfect blend of nature, wellness, and modern living!

Offering affordable rates, a prime location, excellent development, and over a decade of experience in successful projects, these townships are designed to make your dream home a reality. Enjoy a wide range of amenities, including flower beds, a Hindu temple, kids’ fun zones, meditation areas, community halls, and much more for a perfect living experience.
