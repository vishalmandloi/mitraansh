About Project

Spanning 100,000 sq. mtr., this premium development offers residential plots and retail shops with top-notch infrastructure, all set in a serene Singapore theme. From the iconic Merlion Fountain to the grand Marina Bay Park entry, every corner reflects elegance and modernity. The township features unique landmarks like the Universe Statue, a peaceful Buddha Park with a majestic Buddha Statue, and a vibrant Play Zone Park for families. Central pathways inspired by Singapore’s green boulevards add charm and connectivity. Exclusive amenities like a Gathering Palace, temple, Play Zone Park, cricket ground, tennis court, and a thrilling wall climbing area enhance the overall lifestyle experience. With seamless connectivity through 9m, 12m, and 18m wide roads, the township is strategically located — just 4.5 km from Ring Road, 8 km from Tonk Road, 8.5 km from Chokhi Dhani, 1.5 km from Jain Temple, 4.5 km from University of Technology Jaipur, and 9 km from Mahatma Gandhi Hospital. It’s the perfect blend of peace, culture, and modern living — a lifestyle inspired by the spirit of Singapore.

Riyasat Residency and Vatika Residency are exceptional townships in Jaipur, approved by the Jaipur Development Authority and registered under RERA. Offering affordable rates, a prime location, excellent development, and over a decade of experience in successful projects, these townships are designed to make your dream home a reality. Enjoy a wide range of amenities, including flower beds, a Hindu temple, kids’ fun zones, meditation areas, community halls, and much more for a perfect living experience.
