About Project

The Park Residency is a thoughtfully planned township designed with the essence of Jaipur’s rich heritage, following the charming Gulabi Nagari theme. Spread across 10 hectares, this JDA &amp; RERA-approved project offers a perfect blend of modern living and cultural aesthetics. With residential plots and retail shops, best-in-class infrastructure, and wide roads, it ensures a seamless living experience. The township is well-connected to key landmarks like Jaipur International Airport, Ring Road, and Sanganer Railway Station. Residents will enjoy top-notch amenities such as a Govind Dev Ji Temple, community hall, sports courts, parks, and open-air gym, making it an ideal place to call home.
