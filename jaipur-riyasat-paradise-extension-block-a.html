About Project

Spanning across 14,300 sq. m., this thoughtfully planned township offers best infrastructure for a comfortable lifestyle. It features wide roads of 18 m, 12 m, and 9 m, ensuring easy movement and well-organized spaces.

Residents can enjoy a beautifully designed Botanical Garden, a charming photobooth area, a dedicated Kids Play Area, and an Open-Air Theatre for leisure and community gatherings. To ensure uninterrupted water supply, the township is equipped with both an Overhead Tank and Clear Water Reservoir (CWR).

Designed for modern living with a focus on comfort, convenience and sustainability, this township is an ideal place to call home.
