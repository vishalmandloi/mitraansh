About Project

VRB Group presents VRB Raghavam, a fully developed residential gated township. The project is sales & marketed by Gokul Kripa Sales & Marketing Pvt. Ltd. It was launched on 19th Feb'24. With KDA & RERA Approval having RERA No. RAJ/P/2024/3001. VRB Raghavam is situated at Ram Nagar, Kota region of Rajasthan's most peaceful and lovable city of Kota. Keeping in mind the general nature of the state and the city, VRB Raghavam is Lush Green, Polution-Free and provides an environment where any common person would dream to live and enjoy life with family, friends and colleagues. The quiet & peaceful nature of the area is in stark contrast to the hustle and bustle of professional life. We make sure that our customers can build their dream home as well as their ideal life.
