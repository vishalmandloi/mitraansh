About Project

Spanning 100,000 sq. mtr., this premium development brings residential plots &amp; retail shops with best-in-class infrastructure and a serene North East theme. Experience the ease of living just moments away from everything you need, while also enjoying the peace and privacy of your own serene sanctuary.
