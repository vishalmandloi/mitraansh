About Project

Spanning 4.39 hectares, this modern &amp; luxury-themed development offers residential plots &amp; retail shops with best-in-class infrastructure and premium design. Enjoy top-tier amenities like a temple, community hall, landscape park, universe hall, open theater, meditation park, and a beautifully designed valley. Well-planned 9m, 12m, 18m &amp; 24m wide roads ensure seamless connectivity, with Muhana Mandi just 9 km away.

A perfect blend of elegance, serenity, and modern living!

Riyasat The Ring Residency Extension is a premium township in Jaipur, approved by the Jaipur Development Authority and RERA-registered. With its strategic location, affordable rates, exceptional development, and meticulous town planning, this project embodies over a decade of successful experience. The township offers a blend of modern amenities, including shopping areas, a temple, gardens, an open gym, pergola, street lighting, underground electrification, and a secure entry gate with a boom barrier, making it the perfect place for your dream home.
