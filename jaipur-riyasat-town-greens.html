About Project

Spread across 100,000 sq. mtr., this eco-friendly development offers residential plots &amp; retail shops with best-in-class infrastructure and a lush greenery theme. Enjoy premium amenities like a temple, Riyasat Town Hall, landscape park, open theater, pergola seating, skating court, and a solar-powered common area. Well-planned 9m, 12m &amp; 30m wide roads ensure seamless connectivity, with Jaipur-Kota Road just 4.1 km away.

A perfect blend of nature, sustainability, and modern convenience!

Offering affordable rates, a prime location, excellent development, and over a decade of experience in successful projects, these townships are designed to make your dream home a reality. Enjoy a wide range of amenities, including flower beds, a Hindu temple, kids’ fun zones, meditation areas, community halls, and much more for a perfect living experience.
